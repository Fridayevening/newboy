**The market feed is intermittent, not broken. (Corrected 2026-09-24 evening.)**

The first measurement, in the morning, found `GET /v1/market/stream` answering 200 with

Re-measured across the same day, the same endpoints gave:

### Why the owner-token check cannot be finished here

The rejection half was already verified. The acceptance half needs the token the Render
dashboard holds, and that value is not the one in `server/.env`:

```
POST /v1/auth/unlock  {"token": "<value from server/.env>"}  ->  401 {"message":"口令不对","error":"Unauthorized","statusCode":401}
POST /v1/auth/unlock  {"token": "<deliberately wrong>"}      ->  401 {"message":"口令不对","error":"Unauthorized","statusCode":401}
```

The two responses are byte-identical, so the endpoint leaks nothing — that is correct
behaviour. It also means the production value is a separate secret entered by hand in
the Render dashboard and recorded nowhere, and only Yanfei can complete this check.
**This one is genuinely Yanfei's to tick.**

## Decisions (2026-09-24)

Both open questions were put to me and are settled. Neither is a fix.

### The market feed: superseded by measurement

The morning decision accepted the market panel as "intentional design being exercised",
because the server-side feed appeared never to reach live. The later measurements in
"Failed" above show the feed does reach live, intermittently, so **that reasoning no
longer holds** and is kept only as a record of what was believed at the time.

What survives from it is narrower and still true: the local simulation is a real
fallback, it is labelled `Source Local` when it runs, and `disclaimer.scope` discloses
that desktop widgets may be playful simulations. Nothing needs changing — the panel
behaves correctly in both states. What changes is the **open question**, which is now
"why do the providers answer intermittently from Render's address", not "why is the
feed dead".

**Changed:** the two market entries in `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` are annotated so a future
audit knows the failure is intermittent and where the two measurement sets live.

### The cold start: accepted, but the expensive part was not the server

Production step 4 (move off the free plan) is **not adopted** — it costs money every
month and it treats the wrong end of the problem.

The probe in `lib/api/mode.ts` timed out after **3000 ms**. The cold start measures
**24.5 s**. So a visitor arriving after the fifteen-minute sleep could never reach
live mode: the probe gave up, and because every component gates on
`conn.status === "live"` before fetching, the whole desktop opened in LOCAL — no
file system, no news, no owner lock, and the simulated market engine. The ~25 s wait
was the visible symptom; the permanent LOCAL mode was the actual defect.

**Changed:** `PROBE_TIMEOUT_MS` is now 45 s — roughly double the measurement. A
timeout is a ceiling rather than a delay, so a warm server still resolves in ~2 s,
and the probe is the only call that has to outlast a cold start, since everything
else waits for it.

**Not verified:** the LOCAL-mode first impression is inferred from two measurements
and the gating code. It has not been observed in a browser.
